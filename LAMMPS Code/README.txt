Title: Adsorption-Induced Pore Volume Deformation: Implications for Excess Adsorption in Kerogen Matrices 

Authors:  Bo Liu, Jiahui Lv, Saeed Babaei, Longhui Bai, Shansi Tian, Matej Kanduč, Mehdi Ostadhassan

These files include the input and data files for LAMMPS used in GCMC/MD simulations of all CH4, C2H6, CO2 and N2 gases in rigid and flexible Kerogen models at T = 363.15 K and P = 10 MPa.
